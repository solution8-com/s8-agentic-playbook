Wait — I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that: give me a little bit of context, talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, and use the ubiquitous language from `GLOSSARY.md` (or `CONTEXT.md` in an older repo). Keep it short - if the re-pitch is as long as what I did not understand, it has not helped.
